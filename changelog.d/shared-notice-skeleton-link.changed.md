- Pages that fail to load show one notice: `Couldn't load the thing: the reason.` with a **Retry** button that sends the request again. Pages that are loading show placeholder blocks in the shape of their content instead of text or blank boxes. This covers Executions (the table and the Timeline view), Flows, the Flow page, the Task page, Workers, the Worker page, Registry, Anomalies and the triage panel's run list. See [The frame](docs/ui.md#the-frame).
- Links look the same on every page: ink text with a muted underline that turns ink under the pointer. Breadcrumbs stay muted and are underlined only under the pointer. **Show traceback** is a button on the Task page everywhere. The triage panel's title no longer shows a focus box when the panel opens, and still receives focus.
