- A `refresh=true` request on `GET /api/workers`, `/api/tasks/registry` or `/api/anomalies` that arrives while another worker inspect is running now waits for it (at most 7 seconds, without holding a thread) and answers from the result. Before, it returned at once with the registry as it was before the inspect, so a page that loaded together with the sidebar badge could show a healthy worker as offline or missing. A request inside the 2-second interval or a failure backoff still doesn't wait, and a waiter never starts a second inspect.
