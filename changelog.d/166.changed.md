- An `auth_dependency` that isn't a `Depends(...)` or `Security(...)` wrapping a callable now raises `ConfigurationError` at setup: from `init_app()`, `create_router()`, `StemtraceExtension.router` and `StemtraceExtension.init_app()`. `init_app()` and `StemtraceExtension.init_app()` check it before they change your app, so a refusal leaves the app as it was. Before, with FastAPI 0.143.1, a value that wasn't a `Depends(...)` let the app start and every API request returned a 500 error.
