- `GET /api/graphs` reads each flow's time from an index that the store updates as events arrive and as tasks move or are evicted, and snapshots only the listed roots (plus the members of a root group or chord). In a benchmark with 10,000 stored tasks (100 flows of 100 tasks) and a page of 50 flows, a list took about half a millisecond at the median instead of 66 ms.
- A task's time for the graph list never counts as later than when the server received its event, so a worker clock running ahead of the server's no longer keeps its flows on top of the list. A clock running behind still sorts its flows lower. A task's latest time is the newest of those times over its events, and the `PENDING` and `RECEIVED` events of its first attempt stop counting once it has a later event. The graph's own `first_seen` and `last_updated` aren't changed.
