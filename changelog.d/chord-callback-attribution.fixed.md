- A chord shows its real callback or none, never another task. The graph used to find a chord's callback by walking tasks' parents, which drew a later chord's callback on the first chord (a chain of chords on a `celery.chord_unlock` backend), a chord's header as its callback, the callback of another chord that named the same task, and a different callback depending on the order the events arrived in. It now reads IDs the events name (`chord_body_first_id`, `chord_callback_id` and the arguments of `celery.chord_unlock`) and never a parent, a timestamp or a task name. A callback is drawn only when those IDs agree, only one chord names it, a worker has reported it, and it doesn't hang above the chord. Tests check every chord recorded from Celery 5.2.2 and 5.6.3, on cache, Redis and database result backends, after every event, after any one lost event and after any one evicted task. Chords that v0.4.2 drew right and now show no callback: a Celery 5.6 chord whose body is a group (nothing in the events tells it from a chain that starts with a group), a chord whose body is a chain that starts with a chord, and, for workers that don't send `chord_body_first_id` yet, chain bodies and Celery 5.6 task bodies whose callback carries the chord's group ID. Their body's tasks hang from the header they report, and a chord with no callback drawn keeps its `not_run_reason` even when the callback ran. Upgrade the workers to get those callbacks back. See [Graphs: chord callbacks](https://github.com/iansokolskyi/stemtrace/blob/main/docs/graphs.md#chord-callbacks).
- A Celery 5.2 chord with a chain body, and a chord that `chain(group, task)` makes, are shown as a chord from workers that send `chord_body_first_id`, or when their `celery.chord_unlock` task's arguments name the group. They showed as a group, with the chain inside it, because Celery 5.2 puts no chord callback ID on their headers.
- The docs drop the limitations of the old rules: a chord's shared tasks drawn under one chord, and the first chord of a chain of chords on a `celery.chord_unlock` backend drawn with a later chord's callback.
