- The Flow page has a Timeline under the diagram: a row for each task on a shared time axis. A bar starts when the task started running and ends at its last event; a grey lead-in is queue wait, a dotted one a countdown or ETA, and a hatched bar is still running or ran again after a retry. The critical path, the chain of tasks that finished last, is outlined and joined with a line. Choose a row to select that task and centre it in the diagram; choose "+N more" to expand the container. The Timeline starts each bar from the API's `queue_wait_ms`, `runtime_ms`, `total_runtime_ms` and `eta`.
