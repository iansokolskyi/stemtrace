- Adding the members of one group no longer takes quadratic time when each member's first event carries the group ID and no parent, and the members arrive one at a time (Celery 5.2 `chain(group, ...)` headers, `task.replace()` into a group). In a benchmark of such a group, 1,000 members took 7.7 s and 2,000 took 33.3 s; they now take 0.43 s and 0.94 s, and 8,800 members take 4.2 s (more than 520 s before). Groups that contain chain members aren't faster: with 100 chain members and 1,000 other members, the benchmark took 37.6 s before and 42.0 s after.
