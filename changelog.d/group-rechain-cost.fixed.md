- Adding the members of one group no longer takes quadratic time when each member's first event carries the group ID and no parent, and the members arrive one at a time (Celery 5.2 `chain(group, ...)` headers, `task.replace()` into a group). In a benchmark of such a group, 1,000 members took 7.7 s and 2,000 took 33.3 s; they now take 0.43 s and 0.94 s, and 8,800 members take 4.2 s (more than 520 s before). With many chain members (100 among 1,000 members) the time stays about the same: 29.1 s before and 26.3 s after, measured in the same run. With 5 chain members, it is 5.5 times faster.
