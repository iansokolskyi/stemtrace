- Graph API: a CHORD node whose callback hasn't run says why in `not_run_reason` (`header_failed`, `header_revoked` or `header_ignored`) in `GET /api/graphs/{root_id}`, from the first failed, rejected, revoked or ignored header on. Celery won't run the callback after a failed, rejected or revoked header, even while other headers still run. After an ignored header it won't either, unless that task reported its result itself (`backend.mark_as_done`); then the callback runs and the reason clears.
