- `GET /api/tasks`, `GET /api/tasks/registry` and the task-group lookups no longer filter, sort or count while holding the store lock, so a long scan no longer makes event ingestion wait. They copy the per-task records under the lock (a reference copy, about 2.5 ms at 100,000 tasks, growing in proportion to the number of tasks) and filter outside it. Before, the `hostname`, `queue` and `exception_type` filters held the lock for 427 ms at 100,000 tasks, and the default list for 265 ms.
- The graph list filtered by `from_date`, `within` or `to_date` still copies every root under the lock: its first lock hold took 26 ms at best and about 65 ms at the median at 100,000 one-task flows, against about 170 ms before; a second hold of under 1 ms snapshots the page. The graph list filtered by task name, the first flow-time computation after a change, and eviction still hold the lock for the whole store.
- A task list row that changes while the list is built stays on the page with its current values, even if they no longer match the filter or the `sort`; `sort` used to drop such a row. A row whose task was evicted in that time, even if the ID came back, is left out, and `total` counts the matches when the filter ran.
