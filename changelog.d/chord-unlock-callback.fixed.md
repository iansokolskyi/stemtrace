- Fix a chain of three or more chords (such as `chain(group(...), task, group(...), task, group(...), task)`) on a result backend that uses `celery.chord_unlock`, for example a database backend: the first chord now draws its own callback, not a later chord's, and its `celery.chord_unlock` task no longer sits at the top of the graph.
- Fix a chord whose body is another chord (`chord(headers, chord(...))`) on a result backend that uses `celery.chord_unlock`: the outer chord now draws the inner chord as its callback, not the inner chord's `celery.chord_unlock` task.
