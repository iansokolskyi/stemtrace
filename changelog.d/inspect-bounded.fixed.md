- A failed worker inspect now counts toward pacing and backs the next one off (2, 4, 8 ... up to 60 seconds, reset by a success or by a worker the server hasn't seen before). One attempt stops at the first failure and bounds every connect and read, so a refused port, a host that never answers, and a broker that accepts the connection and then goes quiet end an attempt within about 5 seconds on Redis and RabbitMQ. Resolving the broker's host name isn't bounded. Before, `GET /api/anomalies`, `/api/workers` and `/api/tasks/registry` each blocked about 18 seconds on a refused port and minutes on a firewalled host while the broker was down. `GET /api/anomalies` also takes `refresh=false` to judge without contacting the broker.
