- A failed worker inspect now counts toward pacing and backs the next one off (2, 4, 8 ... up to 60 seconds, reset by a success), and one attempt stops after the first connect failure, within about 1.5 seconds even for a host that never answers (Redis and RabbitMQ). Before, `GET /api/anomalies`, `/api/workers` and `/api/tasks/registry` each blocked about 18 seconds on a refused port and minutes on a firewalled host while the broker was down. `GET /api/anomalies` also takes `refresh=false` to judge without contacting the broker.
