- `GET /api/tasks/{task_id}`, `GET /api/tasks/{task_id}/children` and `GET /api/graphs/{root_id}` no longer build or encode their response on the event loop. For a task with 1,000 direct children the loop gap drops from about 85 to 230 ms to about 10 ms. At 20,000 children the loop no longer does that work (about 3,000 operations between turns, was 4 to 5 million), but a garbage collection pause can still stop all threads for a few hundred milliseconds; the gap drops from about 1.7 to 3 seconds. Response bodies are unchanged.
