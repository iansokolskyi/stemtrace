- A `FAILURE` that Celery sends from a worker's main process (a hard time limit, a lost worker) now carries the task's chord (`chord_id` and `chord_callback_id`), as one from a task that raises already did. The steps after a failed chain step also fail for these failures, as they do for any other. If the pool process was stopped after it sent the next step, that step runs and shows as failed too. See [Graphs: failed chains](https://github.com/iansokolskyi/stemtrace/blob/main/docs/graphs.md).
