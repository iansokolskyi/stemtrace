- `GET /api/graphs` sorts and filters flows by the time of the whole flow, not of the root task. A flow whose root ended hours ago and whose child failed a minute ago is now in `within=6h` and sorts first; `from_date` and `within` use the flow's newest task, `to_date` its oldest, and flows with the same newest time sort by root task ID. Each graph's own `first_seen` and `last_updated` still describe its root.
