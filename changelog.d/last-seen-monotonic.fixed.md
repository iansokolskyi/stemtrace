- A `worker_ready` stamped before a listed pid's latest `worker_ready`, or before its recorded shutdown, now changes nothing, and a `worker_ready` never moves a worker's last sign of life earlier. Before, a replayed or clock-skewed `worker_ready` could bring old missed inspect calls back, so the fleet item `no_workers_online` fired while a worker was still sending events, and could bring a stopped pid back online.
