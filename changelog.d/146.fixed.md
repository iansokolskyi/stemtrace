- A group or chord is no longer split when the store evicts old tasks (`max_nodes`). Eviction took the oldest tasks one by one, so a group could lose its failed member and show `SUCCESS`, and a chord could lose its callback. A group or chord is now evicted as one unit: with the nodes drawn directly inside it (its callback, a group nested in it), its members, and the tasks between a member and it (a chain's earlier steps, the rest of the callback's chain). The unit is as old as the newest of them, so one eviction can free more than 10% of the store. A task Celery stamped with the ID of a group or chord the store holds goes with the first group or chord above it, with the tasks between them (in `chain(group, task, group, task, add)` the `add` goes with the second chord, not the first group whose ID it carries); one drawn under no group or chord goes with the group it is stamped with. Tasks a member starts, and a workflow the callback starts, are evicted on their own. A unit bigger than 90% of `max_nodes` can't be held whole: its oldest nodes are evicted as before, and the group's state comes from the members still held.
