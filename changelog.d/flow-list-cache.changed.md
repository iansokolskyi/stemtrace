- `GET /api/graphs` answers faster while events arrive. The server works out the flow times only after an event or an eviction, and then waits at least 1 second (or as long as the computation took) before the next one, so a flow can take up to that long to move in the list. Which flows are listed is always current. On a 2-vCPU host with 10,000 stored tasks (100 flows of 100 tasks), a list took about 4 ms at the median instead of about 73 ms when no events arrived, and about 17 ms instead of about 50 ms during ingest. A worker whose clock runs ahead keeps its flows at the top of the list until they are evicted. See [Graph list time](docs/graphs.md#graph-list-time).
- `GraphStore.get_root_nodes_with_children` returns a `FlowListPage` (`roots`, `related`, `total`, `containers`) instead of a 3-tuple. `related` holds the roots, and the children of a root that has no events of its own (a group or chord); it no longer holds the task children of a root that has events. `GraphStore` accepts `flow_cache_interval_s` and `clock`.
