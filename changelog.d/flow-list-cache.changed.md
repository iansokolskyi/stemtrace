- `GET /api/graphs` works out the flow times at most once a second, and only after an event or an eviction, so a busy server answers a list faster (at 10,000 stored tasks, about 9 ms at the median during ingest instead of about 50 ms). A flow can take up to 1 second to move in the list or to appear. The response snapshots only the roots of the returned page. A worker whose clock runs ahead keeps its flows at the top of the list until they are evicted. See [Graph list time](docs/graphs.md#graph-list-time).
