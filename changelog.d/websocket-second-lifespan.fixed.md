- A second lifespan on the same `StemtraceExtension` (a host that restarts its lifespan, or a test that opens two `TestClient`s on one app) delivers WebSocket broadcasts again. Before, the broadcast loop failed on every iteration with `RuntimeError: <Queue> is bound to a different event loop` and logged without limit. The queue now belongs to one lifespan, and an unexpected error in the loop backs off (0.5 seconds, doubling up to 30) and logs a traceback once, then at most one summary per minute.
